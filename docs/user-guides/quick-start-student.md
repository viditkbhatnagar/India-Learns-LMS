# Quick Start — Student

A one-page guide for your first day on India Learns. The full [Student Handbook](student-handbook.md) has more detail; this is the essentials.

## 1. Activate your account (5 min)

1. Open the email titled **"Welcome to India Learns — Activate your account"** from `notifications@indialearns.app`.
2. Click the magic link (valid 7 days).
3. Set a password — at least 10 characters, with one letter and one digit.
4. You're in. Run through the 5-screen onboarding tour.

## 2. Bookmark these screens

| Screen | URL | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | `/student/dashboard` | Daily check-in: upcoming classes, fees, tasks |
| My Courses | `/student/courses` | Open a course, see modules & materials |
| Timetable | `/student/timetable` | Weekly class schedule (IST) |
| Fees | `/student/fees` | Outstanding balance, instalments, receipts |
| Tickets | `/student/tickets` | Get support |

## 3. Three things to do today

1. **Update your profile** — `/profile`. Add your phone (E.164 format) and address.
2. **Set notification preferences** — `/profile/notifications`. Enable email reminders so you don't miss timetable changes or fee dues.
3. **Browse your courses** — open at least one course and read the description.

## 4. How to get help

- For **course / class** questions → ticket category **Academic**
- For **fees / receipts** → ticket category **Finance**
- For **login / app issues** → ticket category **Technical**
- For **enrolment / batch** → ticket category **Administrative**

Raise a ticket at `/student/tickets/new`. Staff acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 5 working days for most issues (15 working days for formal Complaints).

## 5. Quick FAQs

| Question | Quick answer |
|---|---|
| I forgot my password | Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Reset email expires in 30 minutes. |
| My invite link expired | Ask your admin to resend. |
| Times look wrong | All times shown in IST. Browser timezone doesn't affect display. |
| I paid my fees but they don't show | Wait 24 hours — finance records manually. Then raise a Finance ticket if still missing. |
| Can I install the app on my phone? | Yes — open `{{WEBSITE_URL}}` in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) and choose **Add to Home Screen**. |
| Do you track my watch-time? | No — there is no watch-time or per-page tracking. Progress is measured by completed assessments. |

## 6. Your rights

You have rights over your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

- Access a summary of your data.
- Correct mistakes.
- Withdraw consent for non-essential notifications.
- Raise a grievance with our DPO.

To exercise any right, raise an Administrative ticket with subject `[DSAR]`.

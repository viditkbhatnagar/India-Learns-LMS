## 2. Your obligations

You agree to:

- Attend classes regularly and meet the attendance requirements specified by LUC.
- Submit all assignments, take all assessments, and complete all coursework honestly.
- Comply with the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md) at all times — both online and offline.
- Pay fees on time per the schedule in §3.
- Provide accurate personal information and notify LUC of any changes.
- Treat fellow students, faculty, and staff with respect.

## 3. Fees

### 3.1 Schedule

The fee schedule applicable to your enrolment is recorded on the Platform's **Fees** page and reflects the components in your invoice. Total programme fee, currency, and instalment schedule are itemised at enrolment.

### 3.2 Payment

Fees are payable in Indian Rupees (₹) by the methods designated by LUC's finance team (bank transfer, UPI, cheque, cash, or other). Each payment is recorded on the Platform with a downloadable receipt.

### 3.3 Late payment

If a fee instalment is not paid by its due date, LUC may apply a late-payment notice and (after the cure period set out by LUC) a fees-suspension status to your Account. While fees-suspended, you retain limited Platform access — see [Terms of Service §5.4](terms-of-service.md). Persistent non-payment may lead to administrative termination of your enrolment.

### 3.4 Refunds

Refunds are governed by the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md). The refund schedule appears in [refund-policy.md §3](refund-policy.md). Programme-specific overrides (if any) are noted in your enrolment record.

## 4. Attendance and conduct

LUC sets attendance requirements per Programme. You agree to meet them. Failure to meet attendance requirements may affect your eligibility to take final exams and to receive certification, and may, in serious cases, result in administrative withdrawal.

## 5. Assessments and certification

### 5.1 Assessments

You will be assessed via a combination of module quizzes, assignments, and final exams. Specific weights are documented in the course content on the Platform.

### 5.2 Re-assessment

Re-take and re-submission rules per assessment are LUC's discretion and are documented in the course materials.

### 5.3 Certification

A diploma is awarded when you (a) complete all required coursework, (b) meet the attendance requirements, (c) pass all required assessments, and (d) have no outstanding fees. Once awarded, a digital credential may be issued via the Platform's certificate provider.

## 6. Use of the Platform

You will be issued an Account on the India Learns platform operated by **{{ORG_NAME}}**. Your use of that platform is governed by the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). LUC and **{{ORG_NAME}}** share data to operate the Programme as documented in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) §6.

## 7. Privacy and consent

You consent to the collection and processing of your personal data by LUC and **{{ORG_NAME}}** for the purposes set out in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). You may withdraw consent for non-essential channels (e.g., WhatsApp notifications) at any time via your Notification Preferences. Withdrawal of consent for essential processing (identity, fees, certification) is treated as a request to terminate your enrolment.

If you are under 18 at the time of enrolment, your parent or legal guardian must provide verifiable consent and must sign this Agreement on your behalf.

## 8. Suspension and termination

### 8.1 By LUC

LUC may suspend or terminate your enrolment if:

- You materially breach this Agreement, the Terms of Service, or the Acceptable Use Policy.
- You fail to meet attendance or conduct requirements.
- You fail to pay fees and the cure period under §3.3 has lapsed.
- A regulator, court, or law-enforcement authority requires it.

### 8.2 By you

You may terminate your enrolment at any time by giving written notice. Refunds (if any) are computed per the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).

### 8.3 Effects

Upon termination, your access to the Platform ends. Records relating to your enrolment, fees, and assessments are retained per the [Data Retention Policy](../compliance/data-retention-policy.md).

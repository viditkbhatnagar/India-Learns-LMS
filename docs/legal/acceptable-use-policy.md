This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to all users of the India Learns platform operated by **{{ORG_NAME}}** ("**Platform**"). It forms part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). By using the Platform, you agree to abide by this AUP.

## 1. Why we have this policy

The Platform supports formal diploma programmes and handles personal and financial data. The integrity of assessments, the safety of users, and the security of the system depend on every user behaving responsibly. This policy spells out what is and is not allowed and what happens when it is breached.

## 2. Acceptable conduct

You agree to:

- Use the Platform for lawful purposes related to your enrolment, employment, or assigned role.
- Treat other users and staff with respect.
- Provide accurate information and keep your profile up to date.
- Keep your Account credentials confidential.
- Report suspected security or privacy issues responsibly via the channels in [../security/SECURITY.md](../security/SECURITY.md) and the [Support Channels](../user-guides/support-channels.md) document.

## 3. Prohibited conduct

The following are strictly prohibited.

### 3.1 Academic dishonesty

- Impersonating another user during an assessment.
- Allowing another person to take an assessment on your behalf.
- Sharing assessment questions, answers, or solutions with other users while the assessment is open.
- Using unauthorised tools, AI services, or external help when prohibited by the assessment instructions.
- Plagiarising — submitting work that is not your own without proper attribution.
- Paying or accepting payment for assignment completion.

### 3.2 Harassment and abuse

- Harassing, bullying, threatening, intimidating, or stalking any user or staff member.
- Posting hate speech, discriminatory content, or content that incites violence.
- Sharing sexually explicit content.
- Doxxing — publishing personal data of another user.

### 3.3 Account misuse

- Sharing your Account with another person.
- Creating multiple Accounts.
- Selling, transferring, or renting your Account.
- Using another user's Account without their explicit permission and our authorisation.

### 3.4 Security abuse

- Probing the system beyond the scope authorised by [../security/SECURITY.md](../security/SECURITY.md).
- Uploading malware, viruses, or any code intended to compromise the system or other users.
- Bypassing or attempting to bypass authentication, authorisation, rate limits, or audit logging.
- Accessing or attempting to access data that is not assigned to you.
- Reverse-engineering the Platform or any of its components.

### 3.5 Content abuse

- Scraping, crawling, or using automated tools to extract Content beyond what your role and enrolment require.
- Redistributing courseware, recordings, or materials without our written permission.
- Posting content that infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property rights.

### 3.6 Operational abuse

- Sending spam or unsolicited communications via Platform messaging.
- Using the Platform to host or distribute illegal content.
- Knowingly submitting false grievances or false ticket reports to overload support staff.
- Falsifying payment receipts, invoices, or other financial records.

### 3.7 Legal compliance

- Using the Platform for any unlawful purpose.
- Violating Indian export-control or sanctions laws.
- Circumventing geographic restrictions or other access controls.

## 4. Reporting violations

If you believe another user is violating this AUP, raise a ticket with category **Complaints** in the Platform, or email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** with subject prefix `[AUP]`. Reports are treated confidentially.

If you believe the violation poses an immediate safety risk, contact LUC operations directly and the matter will be escalated to local authorities as appropriate.

## 5. Enforcement

We take AUP breaches seriously. Depending on severity and history, the consequences of a breach may include any of:

| Severity | Action |
|---|---|
| First minor breach | Written warning via in-app notification or email |
| Repeat minor or first major breach | Temporary suspension (manual) — Account access blocked; appeal possible |
| Severe breach (e.g., academic dishonesty in a final exam, security abuse, harassment) | Permanent termination of Account; LUC may also take academic action; matter referred to LUC and, where applicable, to local authorities |
| Financial fraud | Termination + recovery of dues + potential legal action |

Our [Audit Log](../compliance/ropa.md#a10-audit-logging) preserves the actions taken so that users can request a fair hearing, and so that LUC and law enforcement can review where required.

## 6. Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action against you is incorrect, you may appeal:

1. Open a ticket with category **Complaints** within **15 calendar days** of the action.
2. Provide a clear factual account and any evidence.
3. The DPO and LUC ops review the appeal within **15 working days**.
4. The decision is final on the Platform side. You retain any external legal remedies available under {{GOVERNING_LAW}}.

## 7. Cooperation with law enforcement

When required by valid legal process — court orders, regulator demands, law-enforcement requests — we may disclose data and take such other actions as required. Our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) §6.3 covers the data-disclosure side; this policy covers the conduct-enforcement side.

We may update this AUP. Material changes will be communicated at least **15 days** before they take effect.

## 1. What is refundable

The fee components recognised on the Platform are:

- **Tuition** — the substantive cost of programme delivery.
- **Examination** — administrative cost of conducting exams.
- **Material** — physical or digital course materials, kits, uniforms (where applicable).
- **Miscellaneous** — any other fees set out at enrolment.

Refundability of each component depends on the timing of the request and is set out in §3.

## 2. How to request a refund

### 2.1 Eligibility

You may request a refund if you are the student in whose name the fees were paid, or the parent/guardian who paid the fees on a minor student's behalf.

### 2.2 Channel

Open a Finance-category ticket on the Platform with subject prefix `[Refund]`, OR email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** with the same subject. Include:

- Your full name and student code.
- The invoice number(s) and amounts you wish to refund.
- The reason for the request.
- Your preferred refund method (typically the original payment method).

### 2.3 Verification

LUC finance verifies your identity and the eligibility of the request, then approves or rejects within **10 working days**.

## 3. Refund schedule

Refunds depend on when the request is approved relative to the start of the programme. The table below is the default; LUC may publish a programme-specific override at the time of enrolment.

| Time of approved request | Refund of Tuition | Refund of Examination | Refund of Material | Refund of Miscellaneous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before programme start | 100% | 100% | 100% (if material not yet issued) / 0% (if issued) | Per item — admin fees non-refundable |
| Within 7 days of programme start | 90% | 100% | 0% if issued | Per item |
| Within 14 days of programme start | 70% | 100% | 0% if issued | Per item |
| Within 28 days of programme start | 50% | 50% | 0% | 0% |
| After 28 days of programme start | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% |

The Examination component is fully refundable up to 14 days from programme start because the cost is incurred only on actual exam scheduling.

## 4. How refunds are paid

- **Original payment method first.** If you paid via UPI or bank transfer, we refund to the same UPI ID or bank account.
- **Cheque payments** are refunded by bank transfer to a current account in your name. We will request a cancelled cheque or bank-account proof.
- **Cash** payments are refunded by bank transfer.
- We do not refund to a third-party account.

Refunds typically clear within **15 working days** of approval.

## 5. Non-refundable items

The following are **not** refundable under any circumstances:

- Service or processing fees charged by payment intermediaries (none today on the Platform; included for future-proofing).
- Statutory taxes (e.g., GST) where the underlying service has been rendered.
- Material that has been issued and used (uniforms, consumables, kits).
- Examination certification fees once the certificate has been issued.

## 6. Cancellation by LUC

If LUC cancels a programme before it commences, **100%** of the fees paid to that point are refunded. If LUC cancels after the programme has commenced, a pro-rata refund is calculated based on the number of contact hours not delivered.

## 7. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, follow the appeal process in §6 of the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md). For escalations beyond the Platform, dispute resolution is governed by [Terms of Service §15](terms-of-service.md).

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated by LUC. Material changes are communicated at least **15 days** before they take effect and apply only to enrolments accepted after the new policy takes effect.

## 9. Contact

For refund queries, contact **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** or open a Finance-category ticket on the Platform.

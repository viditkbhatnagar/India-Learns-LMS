# Welcome, teacher!

This short guide shows you how to use the India Learns portal as a faculty member. It's written in simple steps — **"click this → this opens → do this."** No technical knowledge needed.

**The website:** open **https://india-learns-lms.onrender.com** in Chrome (or any browser).

---

# 1. Signing in

Your admin will give you an **email** and a **password**.

1. Open the website. You'll see the **Welcome back** screen.
2. Type your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You land on your **Dashboard**. That's it — you're in.

> **First time?** Once you're in, change your password (see section 9).
> **Forgot your password?** Ask your admin — they can reset it and give you a new one.

---

# 2. Getting around (the layout)

Every screen has the same frame:

- **Top bar (blue):** the India Learns logo; a 🔔 bell for notifications; your name; and **Log out** on the right.
- **Left menu (sidebar), in order:** **Dashboard, Courses, Showcase, Grading, Feedback, Timetable, Reports, Chat, Announcements** — plus **Profile** and **Notifications** near the bottom.
- **On a phone:** tap the **☰** (three lines) icon at the top-left to open the menu.

---

# 3. Your Dashboard

When you sign in you see your **Dashboard** — a quick overview:

- Tiles showing **My courses**, **Classes this week**, **Awaiting grading**, and **Students** (across your courses).
- A **My attendance — today** card where you mark yourself: **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, **Leave**, or **Half day**.
- Quick links: **Mark attendance**, **Grading queue**, **Course materials**, **Timetable**, **Notifications**.
- **My courses** and **This week** cards for your classes.

---

# 4. Your Courses and students

1. In the left menu, click **Courses**. You'll see **My courses** — the courses you're assigned to teach.
2. Click a course. It opens the **course workspace** with tabs across the top:
   **Overview · Content · Glossary · Reading list · Gradebook · Students · Announcements · Settings.**
   - Each course shows a status: **Sandbox** (still a draft — students can't see it) or **Published** (live to students).
3. To see your class list, click the **Students** tab — you'll see each student's **Code, Name, Email, Enrolled until**.

**To post an announcement or set an assignment:**

- Click the **Announcements** tab, then click **Open legacy composer**. That page has:
  - an **Announcements** box — type a **Subject** and **Message**, click **Post announcement** (every enrolled student sees it);
  - an **Assignments** section — click **New assignment**, fill in **Title**, **Instructions**, **Due date** and **Max score**, then **Publish assignment**. Later, click **Submissions** to **Grade** each student.

---

# 5. Add content — videos, PPTs and PDFs

You can upload material for the courses you're assigned to teach.

1. Click **Courses**, click your course, then click the **Content** tab at the top.
2. Click a **session** (a topic row) to open it.
3. In the **Materials** card, click **+ Add material**.
4. Fill in the **Title**, choose the **Type** (**Video**, **PDF**, **Reading**, **Link**, **File**), then **drag your PowerPoint / PDF** into the box (*up to 25 MB*) or paste a **URL**.
5. Click **Add material**.

**To add a whole new lesson (topic):** on the **Content** tab, open a module and click **+ Add lesson** — type a title (and minutes, if you like), then **Add lesson**. It's added to the bottom of that module and stays there even if the curriculum is re-imported later.

When the whole course is ready for students, click **Publish course** (top of the course workspace).

> **Big video?** The limit is 25 MB. Upload it to Google Drive or YouTube (unlisted) and paste the **link** as the material instead.
>
> **Content tab looks greyed out / read-only?** You can only edit courses you're **assigned** to. Ask your admin to add you as the teaching faculty for that course.

---

# 6. Grading

1. In the left menu, click **Grading**. You'll see the **Grading queue** — exam attempts waiting for you to mark the written (essay) answers.
2. Click an attempt to open **Grade attempt**.
3. For each essay question, enter a **Score** and a **Comment**.
4. Click **Save grade**.

> Multiple-choice answers are graded automatically — you only mark the written parts. Saving locks the grade and notifies the student.

---

# 7. Feedback

1. In the left menu, click **Feedback**.
2. Click **New feedback**.
3. Choose the **Student** and **Course**, pick the **Level** (Course / Module / Assignment / Assessment), then write a **Summary** and **Detailed feedback**.
4. Click **Save draft**.
5. Back on the **Feedback** list, click **Publish** on the draft when you're ready — **students only see published feedback.**

---

# 8. Timetable

Click **Timetable** to see your weekly class schedule. It's set by the admin and updates automatically.

---

# 9. Showcase — present brochures

If you're speaking with a prospective student, you can show the official brochures on screen:

1. In the left menu, click **Showcase**.
2. You'll see cards: **India Learns — Company Profile** and the **program brochures**.
3. Click **Present** on a card to open it full-screen.

---

# 10. Your profile and password

1. Click **Profile** (near the bottom of the left menu).
2. To change your password, find the **Change password** card:
   - Enter your **Current password** and a **New password** (at least 10 characters, with a letter and a digit).
   - Click **Update password**.

You can also update your personal details and emergency contact here.

---

# Quick tips

- Keep the **email and password** your admin gave you safe — and change the password on your first sign-in.
- You can only add content to courses you're **assigned** to teach.
- Use **Log out** (top-right) when you finish on a shared computer.
- The 🔔 bell shows new notifications (schedule changes, messages).

*Need help? Ask your admin, or contact India Learns support (Vidit).*
